Irrigation problems rarely fix themselves, and they usually get more expensive the longer they run. A broken lateral line can waste a lot of water before anyone notices. A stuck valve can flood a bed. A failed zone can quietly kill plants over a hot week. We troubleshoot sprinkler and drip systems to find the actual cause, then repair it properly.

## Common problems we fix

- **Leaks and broken lines.** Soggy spots, water bubbling up, or a sudden jump in the water bill.
- **Zones that won't turn on or won't shut off.** Usually a valve, solenoid, or wiring issue.
- **Low pressure and poor coverage.** Clogged or broken heads, crushed lines, or a zone with too many heads for its supply.
- **Dry spots and dying plants.** Misaligned or blocked heads, clogged drip emitters, or an outdated schedule.
- **Controller and wiring faults.** Timers that don't run, zones out of order, or damaged valve wires.
- **Root damage.** Lines cracked or crushed by tree roots, often in tight spaces that take care to reach.
- **Valve and manifold replacement.** Old, leaking valve manifolds rebuilt so they're reliable and serviceable.

## How a repair call works

1. **Tell us what you're seeing.** Which zone, what's happening, and anything you've already tried.
2. **Diagnose.** We run the system zone by zone and trace the problem to its source, not just the symptom.
3. **Repair.** We fix it with quality parts and leave the area tidy.
4. **Test.** We run the repaired zones to confirm coverage and pressure, and adjust heads or scheduling as needed.

If your system has more problems than it's worth repairing piece by piece, we'll tell you, and we can plan a [new irrigation installation](/services/landscape-construction/) instead.
