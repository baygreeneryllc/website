A landscape remodel goes best when the same team that plans it also builds it. With design and build under one contractor, the ideas on paper are ones we know how to build well. The irrigation is planned around the plants, the hardscape is laid out for how you'll use the space, and nothing gets lost in a handoff between a designer and an installer.

## What's included

- **Concept development.** We learn how you want to use the space, what you like, and what isn't working now, then shape a plan around it.
- **Plant selection.** Plants chosen for your sun exposure, soil, and water budget, with a mix of structure, seasonal interest, and low-water options.
- **Irrigation design.** Zones laid out by plant type and exposure, so everything gets the water it needs and no more.
- **Hardscape.** Paths, patios, walls, steps, and decomposed granite, built on a proper base.
- **Lighting and finishing details.** Low-voltage lighting, mulch, edging, and the details that make a yard feel finished.
- **Installation.** We build what we plan and stay with the project through the final walkthrough.

## Who it's for

Design and build is the right fit if you're starting over with a tired or overgrown yard, moving into a new home, converting a thirsty lawn to something more water-wise, or have a sloped or awkward space that needs real planning to become usable.

## The process

1. **Consultation and site visit.** We walk the property together and talk about goals, budget, and timing.
2. **Plan and proposal.** You get a clear plan and written scope before any work begins.
3. **Build.** Demolition, grading, irrigation, hardscape, planting, and lighting, in the right order.
4. **Walkthrough and care plan.** We go over the finished landscape and how to keep it thriving, and we offer [ongoing maintenance](/services/landscape-maintenance/) if you'd like us to look after it.
