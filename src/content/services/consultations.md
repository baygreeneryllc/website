Sometimes you need a knowledgeable opinion before you commit to anything. A consultation is a site visit focused on giving you clear, practical advice, whether or not you hire us for the work afterward.

## When a consultation helps

- **Planning a project.** Talk through ideas, priorities, and rough budgets before investing in a full plan.
- **A second opinion.** Have a bid or a plan reviewed by a licensed landscape contractor.
- **Diagnosing a problem.** Dying plants, drainage issues, erosion, or an irrigation system that never seems to work right.
- **Buying or selling a home.** Understand the condition of a landscape and what it may need.

## What to expect

We walk the property with you, look closely at the issue or the space, answer your questions, and give you recommendations you can act on.
