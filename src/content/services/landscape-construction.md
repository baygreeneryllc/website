We install a wide variety of landscape systems and features, both functional and aesthetic. Some projects are a single feature, like a new walkway or a lighting system. Others are a whole set of improvements done in one pass. Either way, we work with you to find the approach that fits your property, your budget, and how you actually use the space.

## What we build

- **Retaining walls and steps.** Timber, block, and stone walls that hold back soil on sloped lots and create usable, level space.
- **Irrigation installation.** Drip and sprinkler systems, valve manifolds, and controllers, laid out zone by zone for the plants they water.
- **Landscape lighting.** Low-voltage path, step, and accent lighting for safety and atmosphere after dark.
- **Lawns: sod and artificial turf.** Sod installation with proper soil prep, and artificial turf on a compacted base for a lawn without the watering.
- **Grading and drainage.** Fine grading to set slopes, shed water away from structures, and prepare ground for planting or hardscape.
- **Decomposed granite (DG) and gravel.** Driveway extensions, paths, and patios with a natural, permeable finish.
- **Planting.** Trees, shrubs, and perennials chosen for the site's sun, soil, and water.
- **Fencing.** Wood fence replacement and repair.

## How a project works

1. **Site visit.** We look at the space, talk through what you want, and note anything that affects the work: slope, soil, drainage, existing irrigation, and access.
2. **Proposal.** You get a written scope and price, so you know exactly what's included.
3. **Build.** We do the work carefully and keep you updated as it progresses.
4. **Walkthrough.** We review the finished work with you and explain any care or watering it needs.

Bay Greenery is a licensed California landscape contractor (C-27 #1146610), bonded and insured.
