Good maintenance is more than mowing and blowing. It means knowing what each plant needs, catching problems early, and keeping the irrigation tuned so the landscape stays healthy through every season. We build a maintenance program around your landscape, not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

## What a program can include

- **Lawn care.** Mowing, edging, and overseeding thin or patchy lawns.
- **Pruning and shaping.** Shrubs, perennials, and small trees, pruned at the right time and in the right way for each plant.
- **Weed control and bed care.** Keeping beds clean, plus mulching to hold moisture and suppress weeds.
- **Irrigation checks.** Seasonal schedule adjustments and catching leaks or broken heads before they cause damage.
- **Seasonal tasks.** Fall cleanups, leaf removal, cutting back perennials, and spring refreshes.
- **Planting and upgrades.** Replacing tired plants and making small improvements over time.

## How we work

We start with a walkthrough to understand the property and what you care about most, then set a visit schedule and scope that fit. You'll always know what was done and anything we noticed that needs attention.
