Not every landscape problem needs a remodel. Often the right answer is a targeted, well-made repair that restores what's there and makes it last longer. We look at why something failed so the fix holds, rather than patching a symptom that comes back next season.

## What we repair

- **Fences and gates.** Leaning posts, broken boards, and sagging gates.
- **Walls, steps, and edging.** Timber, block, and stone features that have shifted, rotted, or broken.
- **Paths and patios.** Uneven pavers, washed-out decomposed granite, and loose flagstone.
- **Landscape lighting.** Dead fixtures, damaged wiring, and failed transformers.
- **Drainage.** Pooling water, erosion, and runoff heading toward the house.
- **Irrigation.** Sprinkler and drip problems are a big enough topic that they get [their own page](/services/irrigation-repair/).

## How it works

We visit, find the cause, and recommend the most practical fix, with a clear price before we start. If a repair isn't worth it compared with replacing the feature, we'll say so.
