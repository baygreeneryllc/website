Yards get away from all of us sometimes. A rental between tenants, a property that's been vacant, a busy season, or a landscape that simply outgrew its space. We bring it back to order, whether that means a thorough seasonal cleanup or clearing everything down to a clean slate for a fresh start.

## Services

- **Seasonal cleanups.** Leaf removal, cutting back perennials, pruning, weeding, and hauling away green waste.
- **One-time and move-in/move-out cleanups.** Getting a property presentable for a sale, rental, or new owner.
- **Overgrowth removal.** Clearing weeds, vines, and overgrown shrubs that have taken over beds, fences, and paths.
- **Landscape detailing.** Fresh edges, mulch, and tidy beds that make a property look cared for.
- **Clean-slate clearouts.** Removing a failed landscape entirely to prepare for a [remodel](/services/landscape-design-build/).

After a cleanup, many clients keep things on track with a [maintenance program](/services/landscape-maintenance/).
