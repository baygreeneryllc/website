This San Jose front lawn had thinned out and turned patchy. Rather than replace it, we overseeded it and followed up with the watering and care new grass needs to establish. The lawn filled back in full and even.

Overseeding is a cost-effective option when a lawn is thin but not dead. It's part of our [landscape maintenance](/services/landscape-maintenance/) services.
