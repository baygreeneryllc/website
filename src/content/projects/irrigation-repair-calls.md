A selection of recent [irrigation repair](/services/irrigation-repair/) calls. Each one started with tracking down the actual cause, whether a cracked lateral line, a failing valve manifold, or pipe crushed by tree roots, and ended with a tested, lasting repair.

Some repairs are routine. Others mean working around thick roots or in tight spaces next to foundations and walls. These photos come from jobs in San Jose, Almaden Valley, and Ladera.
