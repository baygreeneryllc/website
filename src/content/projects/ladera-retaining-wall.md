This Ladera property sits on a slope, and its original timber retaining walls and steps had reached the end of their life. We replaced them with new timber walls and built-in stairs, and finished the level areas with gravel to create clean, usable terraces.

Retaining walls are one of the most common [landscape construction](/services/landscape-construction/) projects on sloped Peninsula lots, where they turn hillside into space you can actually use.
