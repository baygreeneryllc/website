A large, sloped lot in Ladera that had become dry and overgrown. We cleared it and fine graded the ground, setting smooth, even slopes that shed water properly and leave the site ready for planting or hardscape.

Grading is easy to overlook, but on hillside properties it sets up everything that comes after it. It's part of our [landscape construction](/services/landscape-construction/) work.
