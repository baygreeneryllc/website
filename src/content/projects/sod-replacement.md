Sod is the fastest way to a new lawn, but it only lasts if the ground underneath is prepared well. On each of these jobs we removed the old lawn, graded and prepared the soil, checked that the irrigation covered the new lawn evenly, and laid fresh sod.

These photos come from several yards in Almaden Valley, Ladera, and Portola Valley. Sod installation is part of our [landscape construction](/services/landscape-construction/) services.
