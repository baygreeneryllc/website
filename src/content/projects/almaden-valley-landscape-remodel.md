A full landscape remodel for a home in Almaden Valley. The front yard was mostly lawn. We replaced it with layered, lower-water plantings, flagstone stepping stones leading to the front entry, and a fountain as the centerpiece of the new garden.

The irrigation was reworked for the new plantings as part of the job. Like every [design and build](/services/landscape-design-build/) project, it was planned and installed by the same team.
