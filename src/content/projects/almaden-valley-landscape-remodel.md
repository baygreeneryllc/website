A full landscape remodel for a home in Almaden Valley. The project replaced a tired lawn-and-shrub layout with layered, lower-water plantings, flagstone stepping stones leading to the front entry, and a fountain as the centerpiece of the new garden.

Like every [design and build](/services/landscape-design-build/) project, it was planned and installed by the same team, including irrigation adjusted for the new plantings.
