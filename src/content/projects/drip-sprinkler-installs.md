New irrigation systems, from drip lines in planting beds to multi-zone sprinkler manifolds. Each system is laid out zone by zone for the plants it waters, built with quality valves and fittings, and tested before backfill.

Irrigation installation is part of our [landscape construction](/services/landscape-construction/) work. If you have an existing system that needs fixing, see [irrigation repair](/services/irrigation-repair/). These photos come from jobs in San Jose, Almaden Valley, and Ladera.
