This Almaden Valley front yard was mostly lawn. We replaced it with a landscaped garden: layered, lower-water plantings, flagstone stepping stones leading to the front entry, and a fountain as the centerpiece.

Converting a lawn to a garden cuts mowing and, with the right plants and irrigation, can lower water use too. The irrigation was reworked for the new plantings as part of the job. Like every [design and build](/services/landscape-design-build/) project, it was planned and installed by the same team.
